## Context

On November 7–8, 2026, the project's dependencies were scanned with Trivy 0.76.2, with npm audit as a cross-check against a second advisory database.
Trivy was downloaded from the official GitHub release, avoiding the versions affected by Trivy's March 2026 supply-chain incident.
The scan covered all three workspaces (api, web, speech) through the single root `package-lock.json`.
Trivy first generated a CycloneDX SBOM (`sbom.cdx.json`) listing the packages that ship, then ran two vulnerability scans: one on shipped packages only, and one including development dependencies.
Out of scope: the project's own source code (Trivy checks third-party packages only), the separate test-framework repository, and container images, since none are built yet.

## Findings

## Findings

The first scan included development dependencies but showed only high and critical findings: four high, all in development tooling. Medium and low findings were not reviewed before the fix.
A scan of a copy of the original lockfile, made afterwards at every severity, showed the full picture: 25 findings in nine packages (4 high, 15 medium, 6 low). Fifteen of them, all medium or low, were in packages that ship.

| Package | Version | Findings | Ships? | Pulled in by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| nodemailer | 10.0.10 | 5 medium | Yes | Not traced |
| fast-uri | 3.1.8, 4.2.1 | 4 medium, 4 low | Yes | Not traced |
| glob | 13.0.6 | 1 medium | Yes | @fastify/static |
| fastify | 5.12.5 | 1 low | Yes | API server |
| vite | 8.3.1 | 1 high, 4 medium, 1 low | No | Web build tooling, Vitest |

| follow-redirects | 1.16.1 | 1 high | No | Pact (via axios, http-proxy) |
| source-map-js | 1.2.1 | 1 high | No | postcss, inside vite |
| minimatch | 10.2.6 | 1 medium | No | typescript-eslint (API) |

`npm audit fix` cleared 24 of the 25, staying within allowed version ranges; only minimatch remained. A production-only scan after the fixes, at every severity, found none. The fifteen shipped findings were in the live app until the deploy on 8 November 2026.

`npm ls --omit=dev` returned nothing for these packages, confirming that none of them ship.
The minimatch copy that does ship (11.0.5, through `@fastify/static`) was already on a fixed version.

## Decision

## Decision

Releases will be gated on high and critical findings. Medium and low findings are reviewed on a schedule instead of blocking a release.
The findings were fixed with `npm audit fix`, which upgrades to patched versions within each package's allowed version range. Lint, typecheck and build pass on the new versions, and a re-scan reports none. Two R-07 tests that failed during this check predate the change (they fail identically on the old lockfile) and are fixed separately in PR #22.
`npm audit fix --force` was tried and reverted: it moved typescript-eslint to a different major version and offered a further downgrade.
For minimatch, a scoped npm override forcing version 11 under typescript-eslint did not take effect on the existing lockfile. On a throwaway branch with the lockfile regenerated, it did: lint output was unchanged, a planted error was still caught, and the finding was gone. It was not adopted, because applying it means regenerating the whole lockfile.
The minimatch finding is accepted: it is limited to linting, which only reads glob patterns from the project's own configuration, and it is recorded in `.trivyignore` with an expiry date.

## Consequences

- When the CI pipeline is built, the scan runs on every change and fails on any high or critical finding (`--exit-code 1 --severity HIGH,CRITICAL`).
- The minimatch acceptance expires on 1 February 2027, when Trivy raises it again. It is removed once typescript-eslint moves to minimatch 11.
- ESLint 9 is no longer supported upstream. Upgrading to ESLint 10 is a separate task.
- `npm audit fix --force` is not used in this project, because it changes major versions without review.
- The SBOM is generated by the pipeline on each build, not committed to the repository.
- Before changing dependencies, scan at every severity, with and without development dependencies, so the baseline is complete.
- If the lockfile is regenerated for another reason, adopt the minimatch override at the same time (tested on 9 November 2026).
